## What changed since 2026-09-30

141 obligations moved from `Unproved` to `Proved_Safe`: 84 initialization
checks, 40 overflow checks, 15 range checks and 2 index checks. They come
from what 1.6.1 added: membership tests and `for` loops over an object's own
range narrow intervals, array bounds that no declaration fixes are terms the
provers reason about, and a call to a function in SPARK or in a `Pure` unit,
or to a predefined attribute, no longer discards what is known,
initialization included. No obligation stopped proving. All 101 matched
pairs remain AdaLang-conservative, so the matched figures are unchanged.

The previous run's single `Definite_Error`, `test_phelix.adb:41` (`S'Length
* 2` may overflow), is now `Unproved`, and the `Known_Overflow_Failure`
finding that went with it is gone. `S` is an unconstrained array parameter;
the earlier verdict took its length from the index subtype (`FP-088`). The
file is `SPARK_Mode => Off`, so GNATprove never evaluated it.
