## What changed since 1.14.0

3 range checks are added (3 `Proved_Safe`), which 1.15.0 raises on the value a
function returns and on the bounds of the range constraint of a `for` loop or
a quantified expression.

No obligation is gone.

3 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 3 division checks.

No obligation that 1.14.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 184 matched pairs,
against GNATprove's own scope.
