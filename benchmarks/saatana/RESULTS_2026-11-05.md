## What changed since 1.13.0

No obligation is added. 59 range checks of an array value are gone (54
`Unproved` and 5 `Proved_Safe` by an argument about scalar ranges that says
nothing of an array): an array value is no longer given the range check of a
scalar.

Every other obligation has the verdict it had with 1.13.0.

## Bottom line

against GNATprove's own scope.
