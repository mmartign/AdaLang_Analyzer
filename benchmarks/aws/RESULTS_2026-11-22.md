## What changed since 1.17.2

13 initialization checks are gone (3 proved, 10 unproved), raised where
nothing is read: 12 at the name of a formal in a named association of a call
through an access-to-subprogram object named with a prefix (FP-164); and 1 at
an `out` actual of a call through an access-to-subprogram object named with a
prefix (FP-164).

11 overflow checks that were `Unproved` are proved: each is an operation of a
static expression, which is evaluated exactly (RM 4.9, FP-169).

2 initialization checks that 1.17.2 proved are no longer proved: each follows
a call that is now known to reach a subprogram whose effects are not known,
through a call with no actual that 1.17.2 did not take for a call (FP-168),
and what was known of the objects such a call may change is forgotten there.

6 `Exception_Propagation` findings are new in `--automotive`: a call of a
subprogram that reaches an explicit `raise` through a call with no actual,
which 1.17.2 did not take for a call (`return To_Set;` in
`AWS.Headers.Values.Split`, `B := Get_Byte_Safe;` in the HPACK decoder), is
now reported (FP-168).

5 `Uninitialized_Output` findings are gone from every lane: an `out` parameter
that a nested subprogram writes, as an up-level object, is no longer taken as
never written (FP-168).

2 `Infinite_Loop` findings are gone from every lane: a loop left by an `exit`
that names it from inside a nested loop is no longer reported (FP-167).

## Bottom line

No file-level failures, and no `Definite_Error`. No GNATprove oracle for
this corpus (legality-error boundary, unchanged), so nothing here says
whether a verdict is right.
