## What changed since 1.17.1

24 checks, 1 of them the range check of a conversion, are gone (10 proved, 9
unproved, 5 unsupported), raised where nothing is read: 15 at the object a
renaming names, which the renaming does not read (FP-154); 5 at an argument of
a pragma that evaluates nothing, such as `pragma Unreferenced` (FP-153); 2 at
the object of a view conversion given as an `out` actual (FP-152); 1 at the
name of a formal in a named association of a call through an access value
(FP-151); and 1 at an `out` actual of a call through an access value or of an
entry of a family (FP-151).

335 initialization checks are new, at target names (`@`), each a read of the
target of its assignment, where `--verify` raised none (FP-163): 229 proved,
106 unproved.

3 initialization checks that were `Unproved` are proved: the item of `T'Read`
has a value after the call, as has the object of a view conversion given as an
`out` actual (FP-150, FP-152). No obligation that 1.17.1 proved is lost.

6 `Use_After_Free` findings are gone from `--recommended`: a use that the Free
does not reach -- in the other branch, after a handler that frees and raises
again, after the object is given a new value -- is no longer reported
(FP-156).

4 `Uninitialized_Read` findings are gone from `--automotive` and
`--recommended`: an object whose access value or address is given to a call,
or that is overlaid on another one by an `Address` clause, is not known to
have no value (FP-153).

3 `Overwritten_Assignment` findings are gone from every lane: a value a call
of an imported function or of one that may change state returns, or that a
target name `@` in the next assignment reads, is no longer reported (FP-160,
FP-162).

2 `Double_Free` findings are gone from `--recommended`: a second Free of the
same object, which the first sets to null, is no longer reported (FP-156).

3 `Dead_Store` findings are gone from every lane: the value that a call of an
imported function, or of one that may change state, returns and that is not
read is no longer reported: the call is made for its effect (FP-160).

## Bottom line

No file-level failures, and no `Definite_Error`. No GNATprove oracle for
this corpus (legality-error boundary, unchanged), so nothing here says
whether a verdict is right.
