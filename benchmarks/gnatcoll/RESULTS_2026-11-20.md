## What changed since 1.16.0

3 initialization checks that were `Unproved` are gone: in `Result.Set_As_Join
(...)` of `gnatcoll-strings_impl.adb`, the object before the dot is the call's
first actual, whose formal is `out`, and it is no longer also taken as a read
of `Result`. No obligation is added.

Every other obligation has the verdict it had with 1.16.0.

2 `Uninitialized_Read` findings are gone from `--recommended`: `P_C` and `S_C`
in `gnatcoll-utils.adb` (lines 1025 and 1031) are set by calls of the nested
procedures `Next_Pattern_Char` and `Next_Str_Char`, which the check now
follows (FP-146).

16 `Uninitialized_Output` findings are gone from every lane: `out` parameters
whose type keeps a value without one being assigned (tagged, controlled,
access, or with default values), in code not declared SPARK, are no longer
reported (FP-149).
