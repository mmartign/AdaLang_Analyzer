## What changed since 1.17.2

5 overflow checks that were `Unproved` are proved: each is an operation of a
static expression, which is evaluated exactly (RM 4.9, FP-169).

3 initialization checks, 2 range checks and 1 overflow check that 1.17.2
proved are no longer proved: each follows a call that is now known to reach a
subprogram whose effects are not known, through a call with no actual that
1.17.2 did not take for a call (FP-168), and what was known of the objects
such a call may change is forgotten there.
