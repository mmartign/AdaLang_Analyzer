## What changed since 1.17.1

9 initialization checks are gone (2 proved, 7 unproved), raised where nothing
is read: 5 at the name of a formal in a named association of a call through an
access value (FP-151); 3 at the object a renaming names, which the renaming
does not read (FP-154); and 1 at an `out` actual of a call through an access
value or of an entry of a family (FP-151).

Every other obligation has the verdict it had with 1.17.1.

8 `Use_After_Free` findings are gone from `--recommended`: a use that the Free
does not reach -- in the other branch, after a handler that frees and raises
again, after the object is given a new value -- is no longer reported
(FP-156).

6 `Uninitialized_Read` findings are gone from `--recommended`: an object whose
access value or address is given to a call, or that is overlaid on another one
by an `Address` clause, is not known to have no value (FP-153).

6 `Double_Free` findings are gone from `--recommended`: a second Free of the
same object, which the first sets to null, is no longer reported (FP-156).

13 `Dead_Store` findings are gone from every lane: the value that a call of an
imported function, or of one that may change state, returns and that is not
read is no longer reported: the call is made for its effect (FP-160).
