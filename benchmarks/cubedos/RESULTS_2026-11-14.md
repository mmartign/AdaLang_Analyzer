of release 1.15.0. GNATprove was not re-run: the corpus is pinned, and its
output of 2026-10-02 was compared again as saved. Earlier runs are in the

## What changed since 1.15.0

No obligation is added and none is gone.

7 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 4 range checks, 1
precondition, 1 index check and 1 overflow check.

No obligation that 1.15.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

failures.
