## What changed since 2026-09-30

One obligation moved from `Unproved` to `Proved_Safe`:
`cubedos-generic_message_manager.adb:58`, the range check on
`Next_Request_ID + 1`. `Request_ID_Type` is `mod 2**32`, so the sum wraps
and always fits; 1.6.1 models modular arithmetic as wrapping (`FP-091`).
GNATprove has no message at that location. Everything else is unchanged,
including the 667 `Unsupported` obligations.

CubedOS is split across projects, so before the fix nearly every
obligation depended on a type the analyzer could not resolve and was
`Unsupported`. With the imported sources in the name lookup, 635 of those
667 get a verdict, and calls and array accesses that were invisible now
have obligations of their own (190 preconditions, 84 index checks, 928
initialization checks).

Six locations are not paired automatically because AdaLang has two
obligations of one kind on a line where GNATprove has one message. Four
are a precondition counted twice for one call (both `Unproved`, GNATprove
`medium` or `high`). The other two are
`cubedos-publish_subscribe_server-api.adb:351` and `:434`,
`Size := Octet_Array_Count (Raw_Size)`: the range check on the conversion
is `Unproved`, as GNATprove's `medium` at the same column says, and the
second obligation is the assignment of the already converted value, which
is `Proved_Safe` and has no GNATprove message. None of the six is a
disagreement.

With what `--verify` raises since 2026-10-05
(the initialization of each `out` parameter and `Output` global at its subprogram's exit, the bounds of each slice, the range check on an actual parameter, termination, the `Global` and `Depends` aspects, expression functions verified as subprograms and the checks inside preconditions and postconditions), the run has
3,039 obligations: 1,247 `Proved_Safe`, 1,760 `Unproved`, 32
`Unsupported`, no `Definite_Error`. The slice obligation added a ninth
matched pair, `Unproved` here and not proved by GNATprove either. The
actual-parameter obligation is a second range check on three of the paired
lines, which this comparison then leaves out: 6 pairs remain, each one
flagged by both tools. No unsoundness and no false positive.

## Analyzer refresh 2026-10-09 (release 1.8.5)

The analyzer side was re-run on 2026-10-09 with release 1.8.5. It has the
obligations of 1.8.4, every one of them, and decides more of them. A
constant, an `in` parameter and the parameter of a loop hold a value of
their subtype wherever they are read. A call leaves an `in out` actual
initialized, and an `out` actual where the callee writes it on every path or
is SPARK code; after a call of SPARK code a scalar actual is within the
subtype of the parameter, and in SPARK code so is a scalar component of a
record. A call whose effects are not known no longer drops what is known of
the objects it cannot reach. The bounds of an array are values of its index
type, a formal has the bounds of its actual in a precondition, and an
operation of a type declared with a range is checked against the part of its
base range the language guarantees. It fixes two false-safes, `FP-117` (an
`out` actual taken to be initialized after a callee that returns without
writing it) and `FP-118` (an expression function that completes an earlier
declaration read with its formals unbound); no proof 1.8.4 had in this
corpus is lost with them. GNATprove was not re-run: its 2026-10-02 output
was compared again as saved.

## Analyzer refresh 2026-10-10 (release 1.8.6)

obligations of 1.8.5, every one of them, and decides more of them.
