## What changed since 2026-08-29

All of it is `FP-084`. On this corpus many subprograms' fixed-point runs
stop on a Libadalang property error, and the previous build finalized them
with a half-filled reachability table:

- The 73 `Unreachable` obligations were artifacts of that; they are now
  `Unsupported`. One of them, `cubedos-time_server-messages.adb:206`, is a
  range check GNATprove reports as `medium: range check might fail`: the
  previous run's "both flag a problem" pair there rested on a false
  `Unreachable` claim, and now on `Unsupported`.
- 3 `Unproved` obligations in those same subprograms are now `Unsupported`.
- 16 obligations are new (`check_lib_xdr.adb`, `main_file.adb`): overflow
  checks the previous build never enumerated, because its finalization
  crashed on those subprograms. They are recorded as `Unsupported`.

This refresh also caught a regression in the `FP-085` change before
release: it resolved the spec in a declarative part that its exception
handler did not cover (the `FP-080` pattern), so on this corpus four whole
files were dropped (`Error processing ... dereferencing a null access`).
The fix is in the same change; this run has no file-level failures.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness, zero false positives, and no `Unreachable`
claim that the analysis could not support.
