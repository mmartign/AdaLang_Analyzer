of release 1.11.2. GNATprove was not re-run: the corpus is pinned, and its

obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by

The seven "both flag" pairs are checks GNATprove itself leaves at `medium`:
four in `sparknacl-sign.adb` and the postconditions of `ASR_8`, `ASR32_16`
and `ASR64_16` in `sparknacl.ads`. AdaLang leaves all seven `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.11.2

108 initialization checks are raised at calls, one for each object the callee
reads as it finds it: all 108 are proved.

10 initialization checks are gone, at `X'Constrained` and at the name of an
object whose component is an `out` actual, neither of which reads the object.

303 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 272
initialization checks, 18 range checks and 13 overflow checks.

None that 1.11.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,114 matched pairs.
