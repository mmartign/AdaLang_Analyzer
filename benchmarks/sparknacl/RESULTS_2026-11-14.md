A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by
column and leaves no check out.

The five "both flag" pairs are checks GNATprove itself leaves at `medium`:
two in `sparknacl-sign.adb` (lines 488 and 567) and the postconditions of
`ASR_8`, `ASR32_16` and `ASR64_16` in `sparknacl.ads`. AdaLang leaves all
five `Unproved`.

The two "possible unsoundness" pairs are the preservations of two
invariants of one loop of `sparknacl-sign.adb`, on both of which
GNATprove's provers reach their step limit (here and with SPARKNaCl's own
switches, level 4): `for all K in Index_64 range I .. 63 => XL (K) = 0`
(line 604) and, proved since 1.15.1, `for all K in Index_64 range I - 11 ..
I32'Min (50, I - 1) => XL (K) in PRL` (line 589). Both hold. The loop goes
down from 62; an iteration writes `XL (I - 32 .. I - 12)` and sets `XL (I)`
to 0, nothing else, so the elements line 604 speaks of above `I` stay 0,
and those line 589 speaks of are the one line 587 says is in `PRL` and the
ones it spoke of on the iteration before. AdaLang proves line 589 where it
stands, from the inner loop's invariant at line 502 at its exit.
