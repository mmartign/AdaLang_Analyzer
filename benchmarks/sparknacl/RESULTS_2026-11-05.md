## What changed since 1.13.0

No obligation is added. 232 range checks of an array value are gone (229
`Unproved` and 3 `Proved_Safe` by an argument about scalar ranges that says
nothing of an array): an array value is no longer given the range check of a
scalar.

2 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 2 predicate checks.

No other obligation that 1.13.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,114 matched pairs.
