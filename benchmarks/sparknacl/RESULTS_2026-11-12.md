## What changed since 1.14.0

111 range checks are added (96 `Proved_Safe` and 15 `Unproved`), which 1.15.0
raises on the value a function returns and on the bounds of the range
constraint of a `for` loop or a quantified expression.

No obligation is gone.

20 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 18 division checks
and 2 overflow checks.

No obligation that 1.14.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,114 matched pairs.
