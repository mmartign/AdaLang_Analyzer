## What changed since 1.13.2

No obligation is added. 18 initialization checks are gone (all `Unproved`),
which GNATprove does not have: an element or a slice of an object with relaxed
initialization passed to an `out` parameter, which reads no value.

101 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 43 assertions, 30
initialization checks, 10 loop invariants on entry, 9 loop invariants
preserved by an iteration, 3 index checks, 3 range checks, 2 postconditions
and 1 overflow check.

No other obligation that 1.13.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,114 matched pairs.
