## Review targets

The first run identified several clusters worth manual investigation before
any finding baseline is accepted:

- `Global_Contract_Mismatch` on the package constants `R_1` through `R_7`;
- `Wrong_Parameter_Mode` where an incoming value appears in a precondition
  even though the body only writes the parameter;
- `Dead_Store` on explicit entropy-buffer clearing immediately before
  `pragma Unreferenced`;
- `Floating_Equality` inside functional contracts where exact equality may
  be intentional;
- the recoverable `CallExpr` property failure in `Chain_Test.adb`.

These are hypotheses for focused review, not yet entries in
`quality/known_analysis_issues.tsv`.
