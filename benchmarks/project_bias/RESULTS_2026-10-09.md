## What changed since 2026-09-30

101 obligations moved to `Proved_Safe`: 46 index checks, 39 initialization
checks, 11 division checks, 3 loop-invariant checks and 2 overflow checks.
They come from what 1.6.1 added: membership tests and `for` loops over an
object's own range narrow intervals, array bounds that no declaration fixes
are terms the provers reason about, and a call to a function in SPARK or in
a `Pure` unit, or to a predefined attribute, no longer discards what is
known, initialization included.

3 moved from `Proved_Safe` to `Unproved`: 2 index checks and 1 overflow
check (`Chain_Generator.adb:879` and `:953`, `get_time.adb:209`). Each
depends on the bounds of an array that no declaration fixes (`FP-088`); one
of them was the corpus's single "both safe" pair, now AdaLang-conservative.

The rule findings are unchanged in both lanes.

With what `--verify` raises since 2026-10-05
(the initialization of each `out` parameter and `Output` global at its subprogram's exit, the bounds of each slice, the range check on an actual parameter, termination, the `Global` and `Depends` aspects, expression functions verified as subprograms and the checks inside preconditions and postconditions), the run has
2,526 obligations: 712 `Proved_Safe`, 707 `Unproved`, 1,107
`Unsupported`, no `Definite_Error`. Fourteen range checks on an initial
value are no longer proved: the object is declared `Natural range 0 ..
Chain'Length`, and the check is now made against that range, whose upper
bound is not known (`FP-107`). The slice obligation is a second range
check on six lines that were paired one to one and makes one new pair, so
26 pairs were left of the 31; with the later additions the comparison
pairs 37, all AdaLang-conservative; still no unsoundness and no false
positive. On those six lines GNATprove's range check is the
one on the slice, which AdaLang now proves on two of them
(`get_time.adb:209` and `:213`).
