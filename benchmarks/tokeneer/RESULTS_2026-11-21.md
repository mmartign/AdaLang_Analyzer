## What changed since 1.17.1

One unproved initialization check is gone, raised where nothing is read: at
the object of a view conversion given as an `out` actual (FP-152).

2 initialization checks that were `Unproved` are proved: the item of `T'Read`
has a value after the call, as has the object of a view conversion given as an
`out` actual (FP-150, FP-152). No obligation that 1.17.1 proved is lost.

1 `Dead_Store` finding is gone from `--verify`: the value that a call of an
imported function, or of one that may change state, returns and that is not
read is no longer reported: the call is made for its effect (FP-160).

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 451 matched pairs.
