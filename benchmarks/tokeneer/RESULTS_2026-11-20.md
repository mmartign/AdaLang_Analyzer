## What changed since 1.16.0

No obligation is added and none is gone.

4 `Global` aspects that 1.16.0 proved are `Unproved`. The check now also asks
that a global listed as `Output` be written whole before it is read (FP-145: a
global listed as `Output` and read first was proved). These are written before
being read, in ways the check does not follow: `FileAges` of `SetFileDetails`,
element by element in a loop; `LogFileState` of `AuditLog.Init`, component by
component; `ThisTISInfo` of `KeyStore.Init`, component by component; and
`Clock.CurrentTime` of `TISMain`, first written by `Clock.Poll` in the main
loop. No other obligation that 1.16.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 451 matched pairs.
