## What changed since 1.14.0

41 range checks are added (32 `Proved_Safe`, 8 `Unproved` and 1
`Unsupported`), which 1.15.0 raises on the value a function returns and on the
bounds of the range constraint of a `for` loop or a quantified expression.

No obligation is gone.

3 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 2 division checks
and 1 overflow check.

No obligation that 1.14.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 437 matched pairs.
