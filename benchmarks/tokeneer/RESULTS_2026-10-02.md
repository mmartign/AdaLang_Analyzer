## What changed since 2026-09-30

The same 6,697 obligations as before; 138 moved to `Proved_Safe` and 158
moved from `Proved_Safe` to `Unproved`, a net loss of 20. This is the one
corpus where 1.6.1 proves fewer obligations than 1.6.0.

- No longer proved: 118 preconditions, 25 range checks, 9 overflow checks
  and 6 index checks. The preconditions are calls such as `AddElementToLog`
  in `auditlog.adb`, whose preconditions read package state through
  functions (`Valid_NumberLogEntries`). 1.6.0 proved them from facts about
  that state which it carried across the calls and loops in between; 1.6.1
  does not keep such facts (`FP-090`-`FP-094`). The 2026-09-30 comparison
  found none of them wrong (zero possible unsoundness then as now); they are
  withdrawn because the reasoning behind them was not sound in general.
- Newly proved: 90 initialization checks, 18 index checks, 16 range checks
  and 14 overflow checks. They come from what 1.6.1 added: membership tests
  and `for` loops over an object's own range narrow intervals, array bounds
  that no declaration fixes are terms the provers reason about, and a call
  to a function in SPARK or in a `Pure` unit, or to a predefined attribute,
  no longer discards what is known, initialization included.

2 matched pairs moved from AdaLang-conservative to both-safe.

All twelve `Constant_Condition` findings of the previous `--verify` run are
gone. Each was reported from a loop's state before the fixed point converged
(`FP-095`): `if ArchiveFault then` after the archive loop in `auditlog.adb`,
`if not PrimaryOpen then` inside the scan loop of `msgproc.adb`. They were
false positives.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness, zero false positives. 20 fewer obligations are
proved than with 1.6.0, all withdrawn for soundness, and twelve false
`Constant_Condition` findings are gone.
