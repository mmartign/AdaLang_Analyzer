of release 1.11.2. GNATprove was not re-run: the corpus is pinned, and its

## What changed since 1.11.2

1,772 initialization checks are raised at calls, one for each object the
callee reads as it finds it: 1,687 are proved, 85 not.

11 initialization checks are gone, at `X'Constrained` and at the name of an
object whose component is an `out` actual, neither of which reads the object.

854 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 836
initialization checks, 7 index checks, 6 overflow checks, 4 range checks and 1
precondition.

None that 1.11.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 229 matched pairs.
