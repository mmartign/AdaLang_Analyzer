## What changed since 1.13.0

No obligation is added. 294 range checks of an array value are gone (290
`Unproved` and 4 `Proved_Safe` by an argument about scalar ranges that says
nothing of an array): an array value is no longer given the range check of a
scalar.

109 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 86 index checks,
10 length checks, 5 range checks, 4 postconditions, 3 preconditions and 1 loop
invariant on entry.

No other obligation that 1.13.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 437 matched pairs.
