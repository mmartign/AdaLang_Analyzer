## What changed since 1.8.7

1 obligation is added, in code now inside the verified subset, and 5 are gone.
Those gone are initialization checks on the item of `'Read`, which the
attribute writes (`FP-122`).

302 obligations that were `Unsupported`, in subprograms with a dereference, an
allocator or an anonymous access type, are now decided: 143 `Proved_Safe` and
159 `Unproved`.

None that 1.8.7 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 218 matched pairs.
