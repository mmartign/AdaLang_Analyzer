## What changed since 1.13.5

216 resource leak checks are added (188 `Proved_Safe`, 20 `Unproved` and 8
`Unsupported`), which 1.14.0 raises in SPARK code: at an assignment to an
object with owning parts, at an `out` actual, and wherever the scope of an
owning local ends.

223 range checks are gone (223 `Unproved`), whose target is an access value or
a record: such a check could never fail.

6 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 3 preconditions, 2
dereference checks and 1 postcondition.

No obligation that 1.13.5 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 3,075 matched pairs.
