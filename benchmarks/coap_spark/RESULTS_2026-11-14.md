A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by
column and leaves no check out.

The seven "possible unsoundness" pairs are the precondition `Fits_Into
(Val, Natural (Field_Size (Ctx, Fld)))` of `Set_Scalar` in the setters of a
16-bit field that RecordFlux generates (`Set_Message_ID`, `Set_Length` in
three messages, `Set_Option_Delta_Extended16`, `Set_Option_Length_Extended16`,
`Set_Options_And_Payload_Length`), which AdaLang proves since 1.15.2. Each
holds: `Val` is of a type `range 0 .. 2**16 - 1`, the field is 16 bits
wide, and `Fits_Into (Val, 16)` is `Val < 2**16`. GNATprove leaves them at
`medium`, its provers reaching their time and step limit.

The seven "both flag" pairs are six in `rflx-rflx_arithmetic` -- three
postconditions, one assertion and the overflow checks on `2 ** Bits` and
`2 ** Size` -- and the leak check at `coap_spark-messages-encoding.adb:526`.
GNATprove leaves them at `medium` (step and time limit) and AdaLang leaves
them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.15.1

No obligation is added and none is gone. 1 initialization check of an `out`
parameter of a generic subprogram is now raised at the parameter's name in the
generic declaration, as for any subprogram declared apart from its body,
instead of at its name in the body, with the verdict it had.

446 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 229
initialization checks, 151 preconditions, 23 dereference checks, 16 overflow
checks, 11 range checks, 8 assertions and 8 postconditions.

No obligation that 1.15.1 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

No false positive on 3,460 matched pairs. The seven possible unsoundness
pairs are checks that hold and on which GNATprove's provers stop short, said
above.
