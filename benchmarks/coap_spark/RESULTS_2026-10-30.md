A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by
column and leaves no check out.

The six "both flag" pairs are all in `rflx-rflx_arithmetic`: three
postconditions, one assertion and the overflow checks on `2 ** Bits` and
`2 ** Size`. GNATprove leaves them at `medium` (step and time limit) and
AdaLang leaves them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.11.2

109 initialization checks are raised at calls, one for each object the callee
reads as it finds it: 34 are proved, 75 not.

143 initialization checks are gone, at `X'Constrained` and at the name of an
object whose component is an `out` actual, neither of which reads the object.

299 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 278
initialization checks, 8 range checks, 6 assertions, 5 postconditions and 2
overflow checks.

2 initialization checks that 1.11.2 proved are `Unproved` now: the objects
went through an `out` parameter with `Relaxed_Initialization`, which says
nothing of initialization. 1.11.2 took them as initialized on that account
(FP-133). GNATprove reads a `Post` that says `'Initialized`, which AdaLang
does not, and raises no initialization check where such an object is only
passed on.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,868 matched pairs.
