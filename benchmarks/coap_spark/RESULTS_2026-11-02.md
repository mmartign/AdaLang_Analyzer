## What changed since 1.12.2

35 preconditions are raised at operators whose predefined operation a generic
unit hides behind an abstract function, 13 proved and 22 `Unproved`.

9 preconditions are raised at calls of renamings, none proved.

9 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 4 loop invariants
preserved by an iteration, 4 loop invariants on entry and 1 range check.

None that 1.12.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line
