AdaLang leaves them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.8.7

57 obligations are added, in code now inside the verified subset, and none is
gone.

3,375 obligations that were `Unsupported`, in subprograms with a dereference,
an allocator or an anonymous access type, are now decided: 1,933 `Proved_Safe`
and 1,442 `Unproved`.

None that 1.8.7 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,449 matched pairs.
