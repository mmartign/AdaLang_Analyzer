A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by

The six "both flag" pairs are all in `rflx-rflx_arithmetic`: three
postconditions, one assertion and the overflow checks on `2 ** Bits` and
`2 ** Size`. GNATprove leaves them at `medium` (step and time limit) and
AdaLang leaves them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.14.0

170 range checks are added (97 `Proved_Safe` and 73 `Unproved`), which 1.15.0
raises on the value a function returns and on the bounds of the range
constraint of a `for` loop or a quantified expression.

2,241 obligations are added (1,155 `Proved_Safe`, 500 `Unproved` and 586
`Unsupported`) in 12 files of generic units of imported projects that the
corpus instantiates, which 1.15.0 verifies as GNATprove does; no finding in
them is reported.

No obligation is gone.

29 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 21 division checks
and 8 termination checks.

No obligation that 1.14.0 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 3,075 matched pairs.
