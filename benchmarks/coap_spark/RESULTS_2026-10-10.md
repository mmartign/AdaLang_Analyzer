A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER_2026-10-05.md`) pairs by
column and leaves no check out.

The six "both flag" pairs are all in `rflx-rflx_arithmetic`: three
postconditions, one assertion and the overflow checks on `2 ** Bits` and
`2 ** Size`. GNATprove leaves them at `medium` (step and time limit) and
AdaLang leaves them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.8.5

No obligation is added and none is gone.

199 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 108
preconditions, 39 overflow checks, 38 range checks and 14 assertions.

11 that 1.8.5 proved are `Unproved` again: 8 index checks and 3 overflow
checks, in the specifications RecordFlux generates. They had been proved
through `FP-119`, which read a component of every element of an array as one
value. Nothing else 1.8.5 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 1,448 matched pairs.
