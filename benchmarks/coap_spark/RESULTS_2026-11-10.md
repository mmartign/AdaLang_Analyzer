## What changed since 1.13.4

135 discriminant checks are added (130 `Proved_Safe`, 2 `Unproved` and 3
`Unsupported`), which 1.13.5 raises in SPARK code: the selection of a
component of a variant part, and the assignment to an object with
discriminants. No obligation is gone.

79 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 30 preconditions,
17 assertions, 8 dereference checks, 7 loop invariants preserved by an
iteration, 6 postconditions, 5 overflow checks, 4 range checks and 2 loop
invariants on entry.

No obligation that 1.13.4 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 3,075 matched pairs.
