## What changed since 2026-09-30

- **`FP-096`: 628 precondition obligations are new** (430 `Unproved`, 198
  `Unsupported`). RecordFlux-generated code completes most of its
  declarations with expression functions (`Field_Condition`, `Field_First`,
  `Field_Last`), and a call to such a subprogram had no precondition
  obligation before. 89 of them pair with a GNATprove message, all
  AdaLang-conservative, which is why the matched pairs rise from 853 to 942.
- 511 obligations moved to `Proved_Safe`: 486 initialization checks, 16
  range checks, 7 index checks and 2 overflow checks. They come from what
  1.6.1 added: membership tests and `for` loops over an object's own range
  narrow intervals, array bounds that no declaration fixes are terms the
  provers reason about, and a call to a function in SPARK or in a `Pure`
  unit, or to a predefined attribute, no longer discards what is known,
  initialization included.
- 13 obligations moved from `Proved_Safe` to `Unproved`: 6 range checks, 2
  overflow checks, 2 index checks, 2 preconditions and 1 postcondition. They
  rested on what 1.6.1 no longer assumes: the bounds of an array that no
  declaration fixes (`FP-088`), and facts about objects that a call, a loop
  or an alias can change (`FP-090`-`FP-094`). The 2026-09-30 comparison
  found none of them wrong (zero possible unsoundness then as now); they are
  withdrawn because the reasoning behind them was not sound in general.

The remaining "both flag" pair is still `rflx-rflx_arithmetic.adb:79`, an
assertion GNATprove leaves at `medium` (step and time limit) and AdaLang
leaves `Unproved`.
