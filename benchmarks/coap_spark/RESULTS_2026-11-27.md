A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`) pairs by
column and leaves no check out.

The seven "possible unsoundness" pairs are the precondition `Fits_Into
(Val, Natural (Field_Size (Ctx, Fld)))` of `Set_Scalar` in the setters of a
16-bit field that RecordFlux generates (`Set_Message_ID`, `Set_Length` in
three messages, `Set_Option_Delta_Extended16`, `Set_Option_Length_Extended16`,
`Set_Options_And_Payload_Length`), which AdaLang proves since 1.15.2. Each
holds: `Val` is of a type `range 0 .. 2**16 - 1`, the field is 16 bits
wide, and `Fits_Into (Val, 16)` is `Val < 2**16`. GNATprove leaves them at
`medium`, its provers reaching their time and step limit.

The seven "both flag" pairs are six in `rflx-rflx_arithmetic` -- three
postconditions, one assertion and the overflow checks on `2 ** Bits` and
`2 ** Size` -- and the leak check at `coap_spark-messages-encoding.adb:526`.
GNATprove leaves them at `medium` (step and time limit) and AdaLang leaves
them `Unproved`.

## What changed since 1.17.5

Every obligation has the verdict it had with 1.17.5.

12 `Missing_Global_Contract` findings are new in `--verify`: a call to a
subprogram whose Global aspect names a state abstraction touches that state
(`Ada.Text_IO`'s `File_System`, `Interfaces.C.Strings`' `C_Memory`), so a
subprogram that reaches state only through such calls has global state for
which GNATprove would generate a Global (FP-181).

1 `Uninitialized_Output` finding is gone from `--verify`: a loop that goes
round at least once sets what its first pass sets, an out parameter with
relaxed initialization is not checked, and a function called in a declaration
sets the out parameter it is given (FP-183, FP-184, FP-185).

12 `Missing_Global_Contract` findings are gone from `--verify`: a subprogram
that reads only constants whose value depends on no variable accesses no
global state (FP-181).

5 `Global_Contract_Mismatch` findings are gone from `--verify`: a read or
write of a constituent of a state abstraction is allowed as the Global aspect
allows the state, a body is held to its `Refined_Global`, a constant whose
value depends on no variable is no global, and an `Output` global may be read
once the body has given it a value first (FP-181, FP-189).

## Bottom line

No false positive on 3,467 matched pairs. The seven possible unsoundness
pairs are checks that hold and on which GNATprove's provers stop short, said
above.
