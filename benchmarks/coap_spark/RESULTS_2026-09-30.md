## What changed since 2026-08-29

- **`FP-085`: 1,483 obligations moved from `Unproved` to `Proved_Safe`.**
  RecordFlux-generated code states nearly all of its preconditions on the
  package specs, which the bodies previously never saw.
- **`FP-084`: the 65 `Unreachable` obligations were artifacts** of
  fixed-point runs that stopped on a Libadalang property error
  (`rflx-coap_client-session_environment.adb`,
  `coap_spark-channel.adb`); they are now `Unsupported`. Eight of them were
  matched pairs where GNATprove proved the check in reachable code, which
  `compare.awk` counted as "both flag a problem"; they now count as
  AdaLang-conservative. That is the whole drop from 9 to 1.
- In the same two files, 23 `Proved_Safe` and 27 `Unproved` results that
  came from a half-finished analysis are now `Unsupported`, one of them a
  former "both safe" pair (hence 5 to 4). None had been wrong according to
  GNATprove, but none rested on a completed analysis.
- 6 obligations in those files are newly enumerated, as `Unsupported`.

The remaining "both flag" pair is `rflx-rflx_arithmetic.adb:79`, an
assertion GNATprove leaves at `medium` (step and time limit) and AdaLang
leaves `Unproved`.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness, zero false positives. `Proved_Safe` nearly
doubles (1,797 to 3,257), and no result rests on an aborted analysis.
