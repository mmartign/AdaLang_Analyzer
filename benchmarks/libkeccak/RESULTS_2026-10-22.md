of release 1.9.1. GNATprove was not re-run: the corpus is pinned, and its
output of 2026-10-02 was compared again as saved. Earlier runs are in the
Git history of this directory, and what each release changed is in

A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. Most obligations of this corpus are not paired: AdaLang has one
obligation per line of a generic unit where GNATprove has one message per
instantiation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER_2026-10-05.md`)
merges the instances, pairs by column and leaves no check out.

## What changed since 1.9.1

153 predicate checks are raised, a kind of obligation 1.9.1 did not have: 62
are proved, 91 not.

No other obligation is added and none is gone.

158 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 80 initialization
checks, 28 preconditions, 14 postconditions, 10 assertions, 10 overflow
checks, 7 loop invariants preserved by an iteration, 6 range checks and 3 loop
invariants on entry.

None that 1.9.1 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 250 matched pairs.
