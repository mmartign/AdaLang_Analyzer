A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. Most obligations of this corpus are not paired: AdaLang has one
obligation per line of a generic unit where GNATprove has one message per
instantiation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`)
merges the instances, pairs by column and leaves no check out.

## What changed since 1.12.1

107 preconditions are raised at calls of generic formal subprograms, 93 proved
for every instance and 14 `Unproved`.

176 obligations are gone: a call with actuals has one precondition obligation,
at the call, where it had a second at its name.

5 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 3 range checks and
2 overflow checks.

None that 1.12.1 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 252 matched pairs.
