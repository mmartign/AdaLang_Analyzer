## What changed since 2026-09-30

The same 11,172 obligations as before; 407 moved to `Proved_Safe` and 115
moved from `Proved_Safe` to `Unproved`.

- Newly proved: 207 initialization checks, 89 overflow checks, 67 range
  checks, 40 index checks, 2 assertions and 2 loop-invariant checks. They
  come from what 1.6.1 added: membership tests and `for` loops over an
  object's own range narrow intervals, array bounds that no declaration
  fixes are terms the provers reason about, and a call to a function in
  SPARK or in a `Pure` unit, or to a predefined attribute, no longer
  discards what is known, initialization included.
- No longer proved: 84 range checks, 21 index checks, 9 overflow checks and
  1 assertion. They rested on what 1.6.1 no longer assumes: the bounds of an
  array that no declaration fixes (`FP-088`), and facts about objects that a
  call, a loop or an alias can change (`FP-090`-`FP-094`). 66 of the 115
  name `'Length`, `'First` or `'Last` of an unconstrained byte-array
  parameter directly (`Data'Length`, `Data'First + Offset`). The 2026-09-30
  comparison found none of them wrong (zero possible unsoundness then as
  now); they are withdrawn because the reasoning behind them was not sound
  in general.

2 matched pairs moved from AdaLang-conservative to both-safe; most
obligations still fall in the count-mismatch bucket (one AdaLang obligation
per generic source line against one GNATprove message per instantiation).

Seven `Constant_Condition` findings of the previous `--verify` run are gone:
each was reported from a loop's state before the fixed point converged
(`FP-095`).

The analyzer lane took 30 s against 98 s last run: it starts 3,418 solver
processes where release 1.6.1 started 18,670 (see `CHANGELOG.md`,
Unreleased).

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness, zero false positives, with a net 292 more
obligations proved.
