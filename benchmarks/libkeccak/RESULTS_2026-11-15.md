of release 1.15.2. GNATprove was not re-run: the corpus is pinned, and its
output of 2026-10-02 was compared again as saved. Earlier runs are in the
Git history of this directory, and what each release changed is in
`CHANGELOG.md`.

A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. Most obligations of this corpus are not paired: AdaLang has one
obligation per line of a generic unit where GNATprove has one message per
instantiation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`)
merges the instances, pairs by column and leaves no check out.

## What changed since 1.15.2

37 non-aliasing checks, a kind raised since 1.16.0, are added: all proved.

No other obligation is added and none is gone.

81 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 41 predicate
checks, 20 initialization checks, 10 preconditions, 8 range checks, 1 division
check and 1 overflow check.

One postcondition that 1.15.2 proved is `Unproved`: `State_Of (Ctx) =
Auth_Data` of `Update_Auth_Data` in the generic `Keccak.Generic_MonkeyWrap`, a
precision cost of the predicates now taken at the head of a loop. No other
obligation that 1.15.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 262 matched pairs.
