A pair is a `(file, line, kind)` at which each tool has exactly one
obligation. Most obligations of this corpus are not paired: AdaLang has one
obligation per line of a generic unit where GNATprove has one message per
instantiation. The gap ledger (`../GNATPROVE_GAP_LEDGER.md`)
merges the instances, pairs by column and leaves no check out.

## What changed since 1.11.2

166 initialization checks are raised at calls, one for each object the callee
reads as it finds it: all 166 are proved.

18 initialization checks are gone, at `X'Constrained` and at the name of an
object whose component is an `out` actual, neither of which reads the object.

297 obligations that were `Unproved` moved to `Proved_Safe`: 251
initialization checks, 22 preconditions, 7 postconditions, 6 range checks, 6
overflow checks, 3 predicate checks and 2 assertions.

None that 1.11.2 proved is lost.

## Bottom line

Zero possible unsoundness and zero false positives on 250 matched pairs.
